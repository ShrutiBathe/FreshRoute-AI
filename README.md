# FreshRoute AI

### Real-Time Accessibility Intelligence & Dynamic Routing

> **Know the Path. Know What’s Changed.**

FreshRoute AI is an accessibility-focused navigation platform designed to help users understand not only **whether a route is accessible**, but also **how recent, reliable, and relevant that accessibility information is**.

The platform combines accessibility observations, user reports, geospatial information, confidence scores, and freshness-aware data to create a dynamic accessibility map and support accessible route planning.

---

## 🌍 The Problem

Accessibility information is often incomplete, outdated, or disconnected from real-world conditions.

A route that was accessible yesterday may become difficult to navigate today because of:

- Temporary road construction
- Blocked sidewalks
- Parked vehicles
- Broken ramps
- Temporary obstacles
- Changed entrances
- Construction zones
- Environmental conditions

Traditional maps generally provide relatively static information. They may tell a user that a location is accessible, but they do not necessarily answer:

> **"Is this path accessible right now?"**

For people with mobility challenges, wheelchair users, elderly users, and others who depend on accessible infrastructure, outdated information can turn a seemingly accessible route into a difficult or unsafe journey.

---

# 💡 Our Solution

**FreshRoute AI** introduces a freshness-aware accessibility intelligence layer.

The system collects accessibility observations and evaluates them using:

- Computer vision-based accessibility detection
- User/community reports
- Geospatial information
- Observation confidence
- Data freshness
- Historical observations
- Change detection

These signals are used to create a **dynamic accessibility graph** that can support accessible route planning.
